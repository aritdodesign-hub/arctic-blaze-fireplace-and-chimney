Fireplace & Chimney Services

Warmth Built for Alaska

Arctic Blaze is a full-service fireplace and chimney company, run by a father and son. We sell, service and install fireplaces, freestanding stoves and gas, wood and pellet inserts, build complete chimney systems, and repair masonry fireplaces and chimneys. We also sweep chimneys, clean dryer vents, and fit chimney caps, top dampers, new refractory and custom glass doors.

Our Work

Recent Arctic Blaze Jobs

Wood stove conversions, stone fireplaces and chimney masonry: a few of the jobs we’ve shared on Facebook.

Wasilla · Wood Stove Conversion

Gas Fireplace Out, Wood Stove In

New homeowners in Wasilla had a cheap builder-grade gas fireplace and wanted a wood-burning stove. We removed the gas fireplace and the chase it sat in, set across the corner at 45°, then framed a platform, laid pavers and stone with a faux-wood cement mantel, and installed a brand-new wood stove and chimney system.

  • Gas fireplace and corner chase removed
  • Framed platform with pavers and stone
  • New wood stove and chimney system

Southcentral Alaska · Stone Fireplace

Stone Fireplace, Floor to Ceiling

A new firebox wrapped in stone veneer from floor to ceiling, with a cast mantel, a raised stone hearth and room above for the TV. The floors stayed covered from the first board to the last stone.

  • Stone veneer over new backer board
  • Cast mantel and raised stone hearth
  • Floors protected start to finish

Southcentral Alaska · Chimney Masonry

A Tall Brick Chimney, Repaired

A brick chimney running the full height of a two-story home, worked from scaffolding top to bottom. The crew repaired the weathered brick and mortar joints by hand, all the way up to the crown.

  • Scaffolding up the full height
  • Brick and mortar joints repaired by hand
  • Siding and yard protected during the work
